Color 3D print & laser micro-engraving

A personal studio for custom artworks and for decorations in the home and in public places. Pieces are made by color 3D printing and by laser micro-engraving, for clients who value emotional and aesthetic comfort.

What we make

Work is made to order, around a place and the feeling it should hold. This page is an introduction. It is not a catalogue, and it does not show prices or product photographs.

  • Custom artworks
  • Decorations for the home
  • Decorations for public places

Color 3D printing gives a piece its form and its color. Laser micro-engraving leaves a fine, precise mark. Each is used when it serves the work.

A note on support

Friends of the studio sometimes offer voluntary support. That support helps fund a computer upgrade, together with a color 3D printer and laser micro-engraving equipment.

Sponsorship is a gift toward those tools. It is not an investment, and it does not purchase equity, a return, or a claim on future work.
