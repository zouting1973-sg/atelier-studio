What we make

Every piece is made to order. Color 3D printing gives a piece its form and its full color. Laser micro-engraving leaves a fine, permanent mark on metal. The atelier sells finished, personalised goods made with both methods:

  • Full-color 3D printed keychains, figurines and desk ornaments
  • Laser micro-engraved metal tags and photo plates
  • Combination pieces that join a color print with an engraved plate

Order & payment

How to order

  1. Email zouting1973@gmail.com with the item name, quantity, your text or photos, and your shipping country.
  2. You receive a written quote with the item price, shipping cost and lead time.
  3. Pay the quote by PayPal (or through the Ko-fi shop). Production starts after payment.
  4. You approve a digital proof, then the piece is made and shipped with tracking.

Payment

PayPal: after you confirm the quote, a PayPal invoice in USD is sent to your email. You can pay with your PayPal balance or a card through PayPal. PayPal buyer protection applies.

Ko-fi shop: listed items can also be bought at ko-fi.com/timzouatelier/shop.

Shipping

Ships internationally by tracked post or courier. Shipping cost depends on destination and parcel size and is quoted with each order before payment. Typical transit is 7–20 business days after dispatch. Import duties or taxes, if any, are paid by the buyer.

Lead time

Lead times above start after payment and proof approval. Bulk or large pieces are scheduled at quote time.

Refunds & returns

Pieces are personalised and made to order, so they cannot be returned for change of mind once the proof is approved. You get a full refund if you cancel before approving the proof. If a piece arrives damaged, defective or different from the approved proof, email a photo within 14 days of delivery and it will be remade or refunded in full. Refunds are paid back through the original payment method. See the Terms of sale.
