Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

What is collected

To make and ship an order, the Atelier collects only what you send: your name, email address, shipping address, phone number for the courier, and the photos, text or files for your piece. Payment is handled by PayPal or Ko-fi; card and account details are never seen or stored by the Atelier.

How it is used

Your information is used only to quote, make, ship and support your order. It is shared only with the payment provider and the shipping carrier as needed to complete the order. It is never sold.

Your photos

Photos and files are used only for your piece and are not published without your written permission. They are deleted within 90 days after delivery unless you ask for them to be kept for re-orders.

This website

This site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts of its own. GitHub may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.
